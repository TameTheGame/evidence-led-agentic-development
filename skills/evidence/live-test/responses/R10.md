## Final reply

Before I touch it, one thing in the request doesn't match the repo, and it changes what the right refactor is.

**What's actually there:** one area, not several. `README.md:3` — "Its one area is a walled outpost" — and the only generator is `Code/OutpostGenerator.cs` (~100 lines). Nothing else in `Code/` or `Editor/` builds an area. So "the generator every area uses" resolves to that one file, used by the game and by `UnitTests/OutpostTests.cs`.

**Why I'm asking instead of just refactoring:** the file is already short, single-purpose, and documented, and the request names no problem to fix. The plausible readings lead to very different work:

1. **Structural tidy, no API change** — move `OutpostLayout` out of `OutpostGenerator.cs` into its own file, and collapse the `float[] → Vector3` helper that's copy-pasted in `OutpostGenerator.cs:78` and `OutpostTests.cs:19` (probably by giving the layout classes `Vector3` accessors so callers stop poking at `Min[2]`). Behaviour-identical, ~30 minutes, cheap to verify.
2. **De-duplicate against the starter helpers** — `UnitTests/StarterGeometry.cs` has a near-verbatim copy of `BoxMesh` and `AddMeshBlock`. But its own doc comment says it's a deliberate stand-in so the starter tests don't depend on the project generator, so merging them may be undoing something intentional. Needs your call.
3. **Generalize for multiple areas** — rename to an area-agnostic generator, plumb area/layout names through. This is a design change rather than a refactor, and with no second area to shape it, I'd be guessing at the seams. I wouldn't do this now.

My recommendation is **1**, and leave 2 and 3 alone unless you tell me otherwise.

If you expected the generator to place spawns or a camera, that's a feature to add, and it should happen before a refactor rather than after.
