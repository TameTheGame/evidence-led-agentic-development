## Final reply

Done. Six new requirements in `spec/outpost.spec.md`, all `agreed` under your 2026-11-01 standing decision, plus the two cards they need.

| ID | Rung | What it pins down |
|---|---|---|
| OUT-CLIMB-01 | static | The layout describes a climb joining the tower base to its top |
| OUT-CLIMB-02 | engine | Every climb surface base→top has collision |
| OUT-CLIMB-03 | engine | A standing player can climb base→top without snagging |
| OUT-CLIMB-04 | engine | The top has solid ground and room to stand clear of the climb exit |
| OUT-CLIMB-05 | session | A remote client can climb to the top and stay on it |
| OUT-CLIMB-06 | owner | The way up reads as climbable and feels steady to walk |

Each one exists because an existing requirement provably doesn't reach it: OUT-COLL-01 covers *floors*, and a stair run or ladder isn't one; OUT-ROUTE-01 says in its own `why` that it only fits a player along flat segments and that a climb needs a walk test instead; OUT-NET-01 covers walls and the gate, and climbing is the movement most likely to mispredict on a remote client. OUT-CLIMB-04 is separate from -03 because a clear way up still fails if you arrive inside geometry or with nowhere to step off.

Evidence:

The PASS isn't blind — I pointed OUT-CLIMB-05 at an undefined card and the lint went red at that exact line with exit 1, then reverted it. The six `NEEDS OWNER` lines are the only gate output, and no "sentence changed" or "removed" lines appeared, so the nine existing requirements are untouched. The four `NOT FOUND` lines are the new machine checks, which get written after agreement; note `lint-layout` itself is already NOT FOUND for OUT-DATA-01, so OUT-CLIMB-01's rule has to go wherever `lint-layout` actually lives — not in this folder.

I recorded the agreement in `docs/DECISIONS.md` so those six gate lines have a visible authority behind them. Nothing is committed.

**What this reopens once the climb geometry lands:** OUT-SIGHT-01 (new structure near the tower can occlude the top from a spawn — it re-runs by itself), and OUT-SAVE-01, whose SAVE-1 card was GREEN at an older commit.

**Two questions, both product calls I didn't want to make for you.** The six are written form-agnostic, so neither answer invalidates them — nothing is blocked on you:

1. **Which form does the climb take** — external switchback stairs, a ladder, or interior stairs? I'd recommend external switchback stairs: they read as climbable from the ground, which is most of OUT-CLIMB-06, and they give the gentlest walk test. Worth knowing that the spec currently lists interiors as out of scope, so an interior climb needs that line changed.
2. **Should the top stop you walking off it?** A rail is one more requirement; falling or jumping down as a deliberate shortcut is a different one. I'd lean to a rail with a gap if you want the jump-down, but which it is is a feel call.
