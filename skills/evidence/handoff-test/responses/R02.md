## What I wrote

Eight requirements, `OUT-CLIMB-01` … `OUT-CLIMB-08`, splitting "players can climb the tower" into separate failure modes so a red check points at one cause:

| ID | Claim | Rung |
|---|---|---|
| OUT-CLIMB-01 | layout describes an unbroken climb, base to top | static |
| OUT-CLIMB-02 | every surface the climb stands on or holds has collision | engine |
| OUT-CLIMB-03 | a standing player fits along the whole climb | engine |
| OUT-CLIMB-04 | a player reaches the top using ordinary movement (no jump, no wedging) | engine |
| OUT-CLIMB-05 | the top holds a standing player who can move and isn't stuck | engine |
| OUT-CLIMB-06 | a player gets back down the way they came up | engine |
| OUT-CLIMB-07 | a remote client climbs and stands on the same surfaces | session, card CLIMB-NET-1 |
| OUT-CLIMB-08 | the climb reads as climbable and the top is worth reaching | owner, card CLIMB-FEEL-1 |

Plus two cards (`CLIMB-NET-1`, `CLIMB-FEEL-1`) and a short **Player limits** section saying the checks read standing size, step height and ladder reach from the PlayerController at check time rather than hard-coding numbers I'd be guessing at.

`OUT-CLIMB-02` exists separately because `OUT-COLL-01` covers *walkable floors* — rungs, steps and landings fall outside it. I left every existing requirement untouched, including `OUT-SIGHT-01`.

The spec puts **interiors out of scope**, so an interior staircase would contradict it.
