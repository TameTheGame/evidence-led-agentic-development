## Final reply

Six requirements added to `spec/outpost.spec.md`, plus two cards. All `agreed`, on your behalf, under the 2026-11-01 decision in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

| ID | Rung | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| OUT-CLIMB-01 | static | The layout describes one unbroken climb from the ground to the tower top |
| OUT-CLIMB-02 | engine | Every step and landing holds a standing player |
| OUT-CLIMB-03 | engine | Every rise and gap is within what the player controller can climb |
| OUT-CLIMB-04 | engine | A player can climb bottom to top without getting stuck |
| OUT-CLIMB-05 | session | A remote client can climb to the top and stays on the structure |
| OUT-CLIMB-06 | owner | The way up is obvious and the climb feels fair |

Why they split that way: 01 catches bad data for free; 02 and 03 are cheap geometry checks that say *where* a climb is broken; 04 is the actual walk, which is the only thing that catches headroom and snagging — the existing OUT-ROUTE-01 explicitly only fits a standing player along flat segments, so stairs need their own walk. 05 is there because movement on thin, stepped geometry is where client prediction usually diverges, and no headless check can see that. 06 is the one thing no check can settle.

I deliberately didn't add an editor-rung requirement: OUT-SAVE-01 already covers the whole generated scene surviving save and reopen, and the climb geometry is part of it. Same reasoning kept OUT-CLIMB-02 narrow rather than restating OUT-COLL-01.

Nothing about the climb itself is proven yet — this is requirements only, which is what you asked for. I did check the lint can go red: a fixture with a dangling card reference, an owner-rung check that isn't a card, and an agreed requirement with no check all failed for the right reasons, so the PASS means something.

The lint prints `NEEDS OWNER` six times, for the six newly-agreed requirements. That's the gate doing its job; the delegation is what answers it.

Two things to carry into the build:

1. **OUT-SIGHT-01 reopens.** It requires the tower top visible from eye height at both spawns. Stairs, a scaffold or a ladder cage on the tower can break that sightline, so its check has to be re-run once climb geometry lands — its last green was on geometry without a climb.
2. **`spec/outpost.layout.json` has no climb data.** OUT-CLIMB-01 specifies what it must contain; adding it, and teaching `lint-layout` to read it, is the next piece of work.

Two notes on what I left alone: the `## Intent` section still describes the tower only as a landmark, and `## Out of scope` says nothing about climbing down. Both are your product framing rather than requirements, so I didn't touch them — say the word if you want the intent line updated and descent in or out of scope.

I haven't committed. If you want this committed, the repo's git identity is currently `Test Owner`, which doesn't match the `TameTheGame` identity your rules require, so that needs fixing first.
