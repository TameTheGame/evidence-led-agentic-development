## Final reply

Written. Six requirements and two cards in `spec/outpost.spec.md`, all `state: draft`.

**What the one sentence "climb the tower" splits into:**

| ID | Rung | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| OUT-CLIMB-01 | static | the layout declares a climb path from the tower base to its top |
| OUT-CLIMB-02 | engine | every climb surface has collision |
| OUT-CLIMB-03 | engine | a standing player can climb up and back down |
| OUT-CLIMB-04 | engine | the top holds a standing player with room to stand |
| OUT-CLIMB-05 | session | a remote client can climb it and stand on top (card CLIMB-1) |
| OUT-CLIMB-06 | owner | the way up reads as climbable from the ground (card CLIMB-2) |

Two of these exist because an agreed requirement looks like it covers the climb but doesn't: OUT-COLL-01 covers *walkable floors*, and steps or ladder rungs aren't floors, so they'd pass it untested; OUT-ROUTE-01 only fits a standing player along *flat* listed segments, and its own `why` says slopes and stairs need a walk test instead. Hence a separate collision check and a real walk test.

**Checks run:** `py -3 tools/spec_lint.py` — PASS, 15 requirements well formed, *no agreed requirement changed since HEAD*. I also broke a scratch copy on purpose (heading without the em dash, an owner rung pointing at a test instead of a card, a card ID with no definition) and watched the lint fail on all three, so the pass isn't a blind one. Nothing is committed.

**I did not mark any of them agreed.** `docs/DECISIONS.md` has your "make an informed decision on my behalf" note, and I took that for the design calls above — the rung for each claim, the split, the card wording. Agreeing a requirement isn't one of those; it's the thing the owner gate exists to catch, so these stay draft until you say otherwise.

**Not done, and why:** the layout has no climb data yet (`landmarks` gives the tower only a `top` at `[400, 0, 480]`), the `lint-layout` rule for OUT-CLIMB-01 and the three engine tests live with the generator and `UnitTests/`, neither of which is in this folder, and no S&box install here can run them. Also worth re-running once the climb geometry exists: OUT-SIGHT-01 wants the tower top visible from eye height at both spawns, and a staircase wrapped around the tower is exactly the kind of thing that breaks it.

**Three things only you can settle, before anyone writes the layout data:**

1. **Stairs/ramp or a ladder?** I'd recommend external stairs: plain player movement handles them, and they read as climbable from a distance, which is what OUT-CLIMB-06 asks for. A ladder needs ladder support in the player controller, which I can't verify from here.
2. **A rail at the top, or is falling off the point?** Affects whether OUT-CLIMB-04 should also assert a barrier. I'd leave it open — a 480-unit drop with no rail suits a frontier outpost.
3. **Always climbable, or gated later?** If it's ever locked, OUT-CLIMB-03 needs to say which state it tests.
