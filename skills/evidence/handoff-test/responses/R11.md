`py -3 tools/spec_lint.py` → `PASS - 15 requirements well formed; no agreed requirement changed since HEAD.` That second clause matters: I touched nothing already agreed. The six `NOT FOUND` lines under `--sources tools` are all pre-existing agreed requirements whose checks live outside this folder.

**How the claims split.** The one claim I refused to let a cheap rung carry is "you can climb it". OUT-CLIMB-02 measures the rises (cheap, and it names the bad step); OUT-CLIMB-03 actually walks it, up and back down, because OUT-ROUTE-01's own `why` already says flat-segment clearance can't speak for stairs; OUT-CLIMB-04 checks the top holds you, since a walk that arrives somewhere unstandable still fails. Networked stair collision goes to a session card — nothing headless sees it. Whether players find the way up goes to you.

**The decision I made for you**, per the 2026-11-01 note, recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`: the tower is climbed on foot via switchback stairs with landings, not a ladder or a jump route. The stock PlayerController steps up stairs unaided, a ladder would need support the spec doesn't have, and a jump route can't be proven headlessly at all. Rails and looks I left out — yours to call once it's walkable.

**Three things worth your attention before you agree these:**

- The layout has no tower geometry at all today, only the landmark point `[400, 0, 480]`. OUT-CLIMB-01 exists to make the data name a climb path; I didn't add that data, since that's implementation after you agree.
- A stair footprint at the tower base will likely intrude on OUT-ROUTE-01's `gate-to-tower` segment, which ends at `[400, 0, 0]`, and could break OUT-SIGHT-01's sightlines from the spawns. Both are agreed, so building this may reopen them.
- If you'd rather have one walk test than two engine checks, OUT-CLIMB-02 is the one to drop — OUT-CLIMB-03 catches the same failures, just without pointing at the step.

The `Intent` section still describes the tower as a landmark only; changing that line is yours, so I left it. Nothing committed.
