## Checks I deliberately did *not* duplicate

OUT-COLL-01 already covers collision if the climb is built from walkable floors; OUT-DATA-01 covers the new layout data validating; OUT-SAVE-01 covers the scene round trip. Worth knowing: adding geometry at the tower base can regress **OUT-ROUTE-01** (the `gate-to-tower` route ends at `[400, 0, 0]`, right where the climb starts) and **OUT-SIGHT-01** (a staircase could occlude the top from a spawn). Both existing checks catch that, so no new requirement.

## One conflict in the existing data

`tower.top` is `[400, 0, 480]` and `bounds.max` z is `512`. A standing player needs roughly 72 units of headroom, so a floor at z=480 would need clearance to ~552 — past the ceiling. That's why I worded the requirements around a "top platform" distinct from the landmark point rather than assuming they're the same.

## Questions, together

1. **Agree these six?** They stay `draft` until you say so, and you can agree a subset.
2. **The headroom conflict** — my recommendation: keep `tower.top` as the landmark point OUT-SIGHT-01 aims at, and give the layout a separate standable platform around z=400. No bounds change, and OUT-SIGHT-01 stays as agreed. The alternative is raising `bounds.max` z to ~576.
3. **What kind of climb?** Recommendation: external switchback stairs — readable as climbable from a distance (which supports the landmark intent), and it needs no interiors, which your "Out of scope" excludes. An interior ladder would pull interiors into scope.
4. **Is the top railed, or can players fall off?** Recommendation: a rail all round — an accidental 480-unit fall straight after a climb feels cheap. Say the word and I'll add it as a requirement.
5. **Is the climb two-way (OUT-CLIMB-04), or is jumping/falling the intended way down?** If falling is intended, retire OUT-CLIMB-04 rather than me dropping it.

I haven't committed — say the word and I'll put it on `tamethegame/tower-climb-requirements`. The cards have no commit line yet; that gets filled in when there's a build to hand you, since a card on a moving target wastes your time.
