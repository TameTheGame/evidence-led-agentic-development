## Why six are `agreed` and not `draft`

`docs/DECISIONS.md` records a written hand-off from 2026-11-01 covering "the requirements you draft for climbing the tower", ending when the climb ships. That names a scope and an end, so it lets me agree them on your behalf; each one carries an `agreed-by` field quoting it. They're listed above so you can change or retire any of them — that stays yours, the hand-off doesn't cover it.

## What I left for you

1. **A rail on the top platform (OUT-CLIMB-06)?** The drop is ~480 units and the outpost is an early area, so I'd recommend yes — a waist-high rail on every open edge. Left draft because it's a look-and-feel call, not a technical one. I kept fall damage out of the spec entirely: that reads like a global game rule, not this map's business.
2. **Is the FEEL-2 wording the feeling you want?** Draft, same as OUT-FEEL-01.
3. **How the climb is built** — ladder, stairs, or an external scaffold. I didn't decide this, and didn't need to; the requirements hold either way. But it changes how the tower reads as a landmark, so it's worth your call before the generator work starts.

I didn't touch `spec/outpost.layout.json`. The climb's data shape follows from question 3, and inventing fields now would bake in a design you haven't picked. Nothing committed — say the word and I'll put it on a `tamethegame/` branch.
