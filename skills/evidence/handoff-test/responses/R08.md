`py -3 tools/spec_lint.py --sources tools` → **PASS**, 14 requirements well formed, no owner-gate findings. The six `NOT FOUND` lines it prints are the pre-existing agreed requirements whose checks live in the generator and `UnitTests/`, outside this folder — unchanged by this work. I also linted a deliberately broken copy and watched it report an undefined card, an owner-rung check that wasn't a card, a check that didn't name its requirement, and an agreed requirement with no check — so the PASS isn't a blind one.

**Decisions I made on your behalf** (per your 2026-11-01 note):

- **Mechanism left open.** Nothing says ladder, stairs, or ramp — the requirements talk about "the way up", so the generator can pick and you aren't locked in by agreeing them.
- **Descent folded into OUT-CLIMB-02** rather than its own requirement; a broken way down has the same causes as a broken way up, and one check reports which leg failed.
- **No step-height number in the spec.** `OUT-ROUTE-01`'s own note says flat segments only and that slopes and stairs need a walk test, so the climb deliberately is *not* a route — the limit should be read from the real PlayerController, not guessed here.
- **Split arrival from traversal** (OUT-CLIMB-03 vs 02), mirroring how `OUT-SPAWN-01` is separate from `OUT-ROUTE-01` — reaching the top is no use if there's no room to stand on it.

**One decision I left to you:** all five are `state: draft`. Agreeing a requirement is yours alone, and these commit the project to design work, so a standing "decide for me" note isn't enough to flip them. One word from you on each (or all five) marks them agreed.

Two things worth knowing:

- **The layout has no climb geometry yet** — `spec/outpost.layout.json` holds only the tower's top point at `[400, 0, 480]`. Writing the requirements was the ask; adding the geometry and the two checks is the next piece of work, after you agree.
- **The climb will reopen two agreed requirements.** New geometry 480 units tall can block the sightline `OUT-SIGHT-01` depends on and can snag the `gate-to-tower` route, so both need re-running once it lands — their current pass only holds at the commit it was given on.
- **Owner cost:** two of the five need your time (CLIMB-NET-1 and CLIMB-1), and neither card should reach you until the engine checks pass. If that's more than the climb is worth, OUT-CLIMB-04 is the one I'd drop — say so and I'll retire it before it's ever agreed.
