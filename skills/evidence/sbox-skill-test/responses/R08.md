## What the test does

`OUT_SPAWN_01_EverySpawnLandsOnSolidGround` (the name the spec's `check:` line calls for) builds the scene with the real `OutpostGenerator` from `spec/outpost.layout.json`, then for each spawn in the layout:

1. traces a ray down `MaxDrop` (64u) — fails if nothing solid is below it;
2. checks the surface normal (`z >= 0.7`) — fails if what's below is a wall or an underside, not ground;
3. puts a standing player box (r16 × h72, 1u skin) with its feet on the landing point — fails if anything overlaps it.

Failures are collected, so one run names every bad spawn rather than stopping at the first. There's also a guard that the layout actually lists spawns, so an empty `spawns` array can't produce a silent green.

`OUT_SPAWN_01_CheckRejectsKnownBadSpawns` is the "can it fail" half: it runs the same check on three positions that are each wrong in one way — inside the north wall, past the ground over the void, and floating 400u up — and fails if the check calls any of them good. That turns "break something on purpose and watch it go red" into something you don't have to do by hand.

## What I could not settle

```text

OUT-SPAWN-01  engine   not run        needs your machine — card SPAWN-RUN below
```

Three assumptions are riding on your run, and all three fail loudly rather than quietly:

- **Engine API shapes** — `scene.GameTick()`, `scene.Trace.Box( BBox, from, to )`, `SceneTraceResult.StartedSolid`. I can't check these against the engine, so any mistake shows up as a compile error.
- **Zero-length box trace reports an overlap.** If the physics layer returns nothing for a box that starts inside mesh geometry, the room-to-stand leg is blind — and `CheckRejectsKnownBadSpawns` will go red on "inside the north wall", which is exactly the signal I want.
- **Player size.** 16 × 72 are the stock `PlayerController` values. If the outpost's player is a different size, change the two constants at the top.

Also: the surface-normal leg has no known-bad fixture — every block in the layout is an axis-aligned box sitting at z=0, so there's no slope or underside to point a spawn at. It's a guard for when slopes arrive, and it's untested until then. I didn't want to fake geometry to manufacture coverage.
