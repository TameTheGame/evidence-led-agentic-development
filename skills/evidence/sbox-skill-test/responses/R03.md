## What's settled statically

Mapping every solid in the layout, there is **no hole in the floor near the gate**:

| solid | x | y | z |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ground` (BoxCollider) | −800 … 800 | −800 … 800 | −32 … **0** |
| `gate.apron` (MeshComponent) | −96 … 96 | −1024 … **−800** | −32 … **0** |

The apron's inner edge (`-1024 + 224 = -800`) meets the ground's south edge at exactly `y = -800`, and both top surfaces are at `z = 0`. Flush, no gap, no step. The gate opening (`x −64 … 64`, between `wall.south.west` and `wall.south.east`) is fully covered. **So this is not a gap in the data** — which is what I'd have guessed first.

Three things that *are* true and do point at the gate:

1. **The gate apron is the only walkable surface in the outpost that isn't the ground collider.** Everything you stand on is one `BoxCollider` (`OutpostGenerator.cs:31-33`) — except the apron, which is a `MeshComponent` with mesh collision (`:48-51`). So any failure of mesh collision would show up at the gate and *nowhere else you can walk*. That alone explains the report's location precisely.
2. **Suspect in the code:** `AddMeshBlock` assigns `Collision` *before* `Mesh` (`OutpostGenerator.cs:49-50`). If the shape is built when `Collision` is set, it builds from an empty mesh and never rebuilds. I can't confirm that without the engine — but it makes a testable prediction: the **walls would be non-solid too**, since they're built the same way.
3. **Separately real:** nothing bounds the apron's south edge at `y = -1024`. Past it there is no floor at all. Walk out of the gate and keep going and you drop. That could be the entire report on its own, with no engine bug.

The tester reporting only the floor — not the walls — mildly favours (3) over (2), but they may simply not have tried the walls.

## The check that should have caught this didn't exist

`OUT-COLL-01` is `agreed` and names `OUT_COLL_01_EveryFloorHasCollision`. It was never written. I've written it: `UnitTests/CollisionTests.cs`. It builds the scene the way the game does, then traces down onto every floor surface and the listed routes. Two deliberate design points:
