## Final reply

## What's wrong

The layout data is fine — I ruled that out first, since it was the cheap check. The gate apron (x −96…96, y −1024…−800, top z=0) meets the ground (top z=0) exactly at y=−800, right under the gate gap. No hole.

The bug is in `Code/OutpostGenerator.cs:42` — `AddMeshBlock` creates the `MeshComponent` **enabled**, then assigns `Collision` and `Mesh`. Per this project's engine reference, a `MeshComponent` in a game scene builds its model only as it's enabled, so a `Mesh` assigned afterwards is ignored. The block gets no geometry and no collision.

The apron is the only walkable floor built as a mesh block — everywhere else you walk is the ground's `BoxCollider`, which is unaffected. That's why the symptom appears *at the gate* specifically: step past y=−800 and there's nothing under you.

## What I changed

- `Code/OutpostGenerator.cs` — create disabled, assign, then enable.
- `UnitTests/CollisionTests.cs` (new) — `OUT_COLL_01_EveryFloorHasCollision`, the check `spec/outpost.spec.md` already names for OUT-COLL-01. It rebuilds the scene from the layout and traces down onto a 64-unit grid over every walkable floor, skipping spots where a wall or the tower stands. Floors are derived from the data alone (the ground top, plus any block topping out at ground height), not from the generator, so the generator isn't grading itself.
