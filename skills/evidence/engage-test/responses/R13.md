## One risk worth knowing before you run the tests

The tower is at `[400, 0]` — the east half of the outpost is where the landmark already lives. `spawn.east` therefore sits on the tower's own axis, 200 units from its base, giving a near-vertical sightline (~64°) up to the tank top at `z: 480`. A water tower's tank overhangs its legs, so the tank may occlude its own top point from that close. If **OUT_SIGHT_01_TowerVisibleFromSpawns** goes red, that's the cause, and the fix is to nudge the `y` value (e.g. `[600, 300, 8]`) rather than to move it east into the wall. I'd rather flag this than quietly pick an asymmetric position you didn't ask for.
