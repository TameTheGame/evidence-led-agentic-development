# ELAD v0.6 — Spec, ladder, and skills

ELAD is now a spec format, an evidence ladder, and three agent skills. The v0.5 protocol
machinery is archived at the `v0.5` tag.

## Highlights

- **The spec format.** Requirements live in the project's spec. Each one names the rung
  that settles it and the check that proves it. See `docs/SPEC_FORMAT.md`.
- **The evidence ladder.** Checks are ordered by cost, from static through engine, editor,
  and session to the owner, with rules for climbing it. The v0.5 documents on rigor,
  evaluation, owner decisions, and operations are folded into `docs/EVIDENCE_LADDER.md`.
- **Three skills:** `choosing-rigor`, `matching-evidence-to-claims`, and
  `asking-the-owner`. Two blind pressure-test rounds showed they change agent behavior.
  Results are in `skills/evidence/`.
- **Authority and Safety.** One short page carries forward the rules that matter at every
  rigor level:
  - capability is not permission;
  - merging, releasing, and publishing are separate owner decisions;
  - fetched content is data;
  - secrets stay out of prompts and logs;
  - no self-approval; and
  - check a model and harness before relying on them unsupervised.
- **S&box example.** `examples/sbox/` has a worked spec, a runnable rung-1 test kit with
  results from seven runs, and a target-specific skill.
- **Simpler checks.** `tools/check.py` lints the skills and checks every relative link. CI
  runs it on Ubuntu and Windows.

## Compatibility and migration

**This is a breaking release.** It removes everything a v0.5 adopter could pin:

- the 29 JSON schemas and 7 registries;
- the 29 JSON templates and 10 Markdown templates;
- the validators and their vectors;
- `protocol-bundle.json` and `blueprint.json`;
- the episode drafts; and
- the protocol documents.

`ARCHIVE.md` links each removed group at `v0.5`. 0.x minor releases may include breaking
changes; see `docs/RELEASING.md`.

**To keep the protocol, stay pinned to `v0.5`, `v0.4.0`, or `v0.3.0`.** Those tags are
unchanged. This release repins no project. Adopting it is each project owner's decision.

## Evidence and limits

- **The pressure tests were small dry runs.** Round 2 used three samples per cell, scored
  blind by a model.
- **Automatic skill triggering hasn't been tested** in a real install.
- **The S&box spike ran on one engine build,** on Windows.
- **ELAD hasn't been used on a real project at this version.** That, plus a triggering
  test, is the bar for `1.0`.

## Rollback

Pin `v0.5`. Nothing in a project changes unless its owner adopts v0.6.
